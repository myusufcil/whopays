Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Who Pays? uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Who Pays? YouTube channel. It uses Google OAuth to upload videos to, and read statistics of, the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Data accessed: the channel owner's YouTube channel information, videos and analytics. No data of other users is collected.

Use: data is used only to publish and schedule the channel's videos and to measure their performance.

Storage and sharing: OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computers. No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
